How to play
  1. Press Start. One pad lights up: tap it.
  2. Each round replays the whole pattern and adds one more pad. Repeat it in the same order.
  3. One wrong pad ends the game. The pattern gets faster as you go.
  4. On a keyboard, use Q, W, A and S for the four pads, and Space or Enter to start.
